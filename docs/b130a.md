# BlackVue B-130A

Status: hardware-observed telemetry on firmware **1.61**, returned identity **B-130A**. The [shared wire protocol](blackvue-legacy.md) applies, with big-endian input/output voltage at offsets 23 and 25 instead of classic offsets 11 and 12.

## Hardware evidence

The October 10, 2026 dataset contains 346 full status frames in the successful session: 215 discharging, 128 charging and three stopped during transition. Status replies were 29 bytes with payload length 24. Two identity replies in that session returned revision 161 and model B-130A.

- Charge 54–57%; temperature 27–28°C.
- Charging current 9 A, with 23–21 minutes to full.
- Discharge output 12.7–13.1 V / 1.041–1.308 A.
- Observed discharge → charge → stopped → discharge transition.
- Reported configured capacity 7,500 mAh.

Manufacturer screenshots corroborated 57%, 28°C, 9.00 charging current and 21 minutes to full. A later discharge display showed 12.9 V / 1.03 and 4h03m; the last captured sample showed 13.0 V / 1.099 A and a raw 3h44m estimate. Preserve both observations without forcing exact asynchronous agreement.

## Identity and connection lessons

An advertised name can begin `130A` without a leading `B`. Requiring the manufacturer source's `B-130X` identity rejected the tested pack's valid `B-130A` reply and prevented status requests. Accept the evidenced model within its selected family, not a guessed substitute.

One write operation returned GATT error 133, followed by disconnect status 8. A new connection resumed identity/status collection. That demonstrates recovery after reconnection, not automatic reconnection or uninterrupted monitoring. This successful session started with the car off; an initial engine-on B-130A connection is not established by this dataset.

## Limits

B-130X identity handling is source-derived; no separate B-130X hardware validation is claimed. Extended flags, complete protection states, incoming checksum semantics and exact manufacturer runtime smoothing remain unverified. Configured capacity does not establish usable capacity or battery health.
