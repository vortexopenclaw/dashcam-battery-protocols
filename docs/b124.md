# BlackVue B-124

Status: hardware-observed telemetry on firmware **1.44**, returned identity **B-124**. The [shared wire protocol](blackvue-legacy.md) defines UUIDs, requests, framing and offsets. This revision uses the classic input/output voltage bytes at offsets 11 and 12.

## Hardware evidence

The October 10, 2026 dataset contains 487 decoded full status frames and three firmware identity frames across B-124 sessions. Earlier discovery-only data also contains 801 repeated connection-ready frames with no telemetry; those are not status samples.

- Charging and discharging, including charging input with a simultaneous output load.
- Charge readings from 47% to 84% across sessions; positive temperature and electrical scales corroborated by manufacturer screenshots.
- Connection while already charging, followed by a completed capture/upload without recorded Bluetooth operation errors in that sample.

A charging sample reported 83–84%, 28°C, 13.6–13.8 V / 9 A input and 13.7–13.8 V / 0.480–0.562 A output. Calculated powers were about 122–124 W input and 6.6–7.7 W output. Time to full was 14–16 minutes. The manufacturer display showed 84%, 28°C, 13.8 V / 9.00, 13.8 V / 0.52 and 14 minutes, a ballpark match.

A discharge comparison corroborated 75%, 29°C, 13.2 V and no charging input. The raw runtime estimate differed from the later manufacturer display; exact UI estimate matching is outside this reference's validation claim.

## Limits

The tested configuration reported **12,600 mAh**. That is a device-reported configured value, not a model-wide nominal capacity, measured usable capacity or proof of expansion hardware. B-124X firmware identities appear in the manufacturer source but have no separate hardware capture here. Older-firmware branches and full-charge/protection states remain source-derived or unknown.
